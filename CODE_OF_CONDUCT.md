# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We aim to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever
their background, first language, or how much they agree with our level assignments.

## Standards

Behaviours that help us:

* criticise the measurement, never the measurer;
* bring evidence — a corpus count, a published profile, a reproducible script;
* accept that CEFR levels are professional judgement calls and disagree politely;
* keep issues and PRs about the data, not about people.

Unacceptable: personal attacks, gatekeeping by native-speaker status, unsolicited "corrections"
without evidence, harassment, or publishing another contributor's contact or account details.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions) and in public spaces when an
individual represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers via a private advisory (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Reports are
reviewed promptly and fairly; enforcement ranges from a warning to removal of access, following
the Contributor Covenant 2.1 escalation model.
